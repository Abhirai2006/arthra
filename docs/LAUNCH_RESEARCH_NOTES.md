# Launch Research Notes

## Privacy and governance source

On 24 August 2026, the official Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology publication of **The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** was reviewed as the primary source for Arthra’s India-focused privacy-governance checklist.[1]

This project documentation does **not** claim that Arthra is compliant with the Act or any other law. The owner must confirm the controller/operator identity, applicable jurisdiction, actual data flows, processor list, retention rules, grievance contact, and effective dates with qualified counsel before public launch. The product changes in this release provide an owner-only way to review and delete consented Contact and Waitlist records; they do not substitute for legal review or an operational privacy program.
